WHAT THE PRODUCT IS DOING

Low-risk rules

01 · FOR THE FARMER

Familiar entry. Useful answer.

A voice note, photo or simple question becomes a case with clear next steps. We reduce the effort of finding the right person.

02 · FOR THE FPO / KVK

One queue. Clear ownership.

Tagged requests, missing context, expert review and market inquiries become trackable instead of disappearing into chat history.

03 · FOR THE ECOSYSTEM

Measure outcomes, not AI chatter.

Track resolution time, correct eligibility checks, verified buyer matches and farmer net realization—not chatbot messages alone.
